System Overview
Since 1996 Scorpos has been providing powerful enterprise resource planning (ERP) software solutions that enable clients to operate more efficiently and more profitable than ever before, based upon decades of functional experience and technological expertise, Scorpos has developed the most comprehensive and effective ERP solutions named SEAS® and is the flagship product of Scorpos. SEAS® offers flexible, scalable performance and a strong base from which to Maximize Profit, Improve Efficiency & Improve Customer Satisfaction.

SEAS® automates and streamlines your enterprise planning and shop floor operations by providing critical information on every level of the manufacturing process – from resource planning to shop floor to administration. SEAS® helps drive decisions in sales, finance and customer support. Now your entire organization works in sync with up-to-the minute customer information to serve your customers better and to enhance your bottom line.
