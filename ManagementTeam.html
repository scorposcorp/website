The Driving Force
  S. Srinivas
Chief Executive Officer
 
S. Srinivas is the Founder-Promoter of Scorpos Corporate Services. As Chief Executive Officer, he plays a key role in defining the company strategy and in using technology and innovation continuously to maintain its leadership in the industry.
His initial responsibilities included management of design, development, implementation and support of information systems for clients in the manufacturing industry. It is his unmatched contribution that led to the success of several projects undertaken by the company. He presently looks after the strategy formulation, business development, identifying emerging markets, building alliances with various software majors & oversees the operations of the organization.
Srinivas holds an M.Tech (Computer Science) from Osmania University in the year 1985. He began his career at Bajaj Auto Limited, with the MIS department in the year 1986 and was heading the IT division within a short span of time. Srinivas has also held responsible positions with Raipur Alloys & Steel Limited and was associated with various IT organizations in India and abroad, before he incorporated SCORPOS in the year 1996 at Hyderabad.
