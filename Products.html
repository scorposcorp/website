  Equipment Warranty Management System
ScorP-EWMS is vital aspect of the any Manufacturing Organization in terms of its product performance and Warranty liability. The system facilitates for Warranty Claim processing and keeps track of the complete history of the products including the warranty parameters specified by the organization. Warranty claims are processed by the service personnel at the OEM / Dealer site by checking history of the product. Inspection and validation on the warranty claims are done and the defective parts are received at the factory for the failure analysis. Warranty / Quality Dept ascertain the exact cause of the failure and attributes to the process defect / Material defect. The system is integrated with Financial Accounting for settlement and reconciliation. The system also provides for Indenting / drawing the requisite material against the warranty requirements.
