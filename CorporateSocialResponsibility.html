Corporate Social Responsibility
 


 
 
   
 
Scorpos always encourages and motivates its employees to do something for the welfare of the needy. It is, in fact, an initiative that is to help enrich the quality of life of the community and preserve ecological balance and heritage through a strong environment conscience in society.
We believe that contributing to the well-being and development of society is an extension of everything we do. Scorpos also aims to conserve energy and better our environment through planned approach. We are taking strides to conserve energy and better our environment by using computer technology and resources wisely. Scorpos is becoming more energy efficient while using renewable resources in a sustainable manner.
Our Aim
To inspire and motivate people around us by effective use of our resources in a rational manner and with humility.
Focus Areas
  • Education
  • Environment
  • Computer awareness
At Scorpos, we endeavor to make a positive contribution to the underprivileged by supporting a wide range of educational and socio economic initiatives. Community projects and programs are driven by active participation from Scorpians.
