Scorpos Corporate Services (P) Limited is a leading Global Consulting & IT Services company offering a wide array of solutions customized for a range of key verticals and horizontals. Established in the year 1996, Scorpos has achieved success in all the areas of its operations. As a diverse end-to-end IT solutions provider, we offer our expertise aimed at helping customers re-engineer and re-invent their businesses to compete successfully in an ever-changing global scenario. Our final objective is providing our clients the competitive edge in the market-place.

The business theme of our organization is, “Quality Service and Support People in IT”, that aptly signifies the synergies involved in our operations. Scorpos has extensive domain competencies in verticals such as Manufacturing, BFSI, Telecom, Retail & FMCG, Life Sciences & Healthcare, Hospitality & Leisure, Logistics, Agro based Industry & Education. We have proven strengths in identifying Organizational Requirements, establishing Systems & Procedures, Requirement Definition Study, System Analysis & Design, and Development of web-based applications & custom ERP’s

We have pioneered the development of Applications using MVC – II architecture. We have a strong team of committed professionals with vast experience in the latest cutting edge technologies. Our strong framework of processes, tools and techniques, implemented through well-defined and robust framework of procedures form the basis of all delivery activities.
