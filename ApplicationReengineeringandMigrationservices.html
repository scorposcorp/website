Application Reengineering and Migration
To ensure integration, versatility, transparency and secure collaboration of software development projects Scorpos uses a 100% Web-based solution that drives the creation of software by distributed development teams.

Legacy software, applications that have been in use by a company for many years and that support the unique processes of the organization. With the Technology advances, Business scenarios change. Existing legacy software systems need to move with the times too, evolving into state-of-the-art IT solutions that cater to expanding business needs, while preserving established business rules and practices coded in the old system.

Scorpos Application Reengineering and Migration, using state-of-the-art technology environments, transforms legacy software into high-performance, easy to use applications designed to respond to growing business needs.
Our Application Re-engineering and Migration services usually include the following:
Our Re-engineering comes with several options and some of the familiar ones are listed below; each one has its own business justification and commercial benefits:
  • Re-engineering at Code/Design Level
  • Integrate with other application and products
  • Replace of the Components (Data Base , User Interface)
  • Re-development
  • Assessment, Documentation
  • Code Splitting and Optimization
  • Down Sizing and Right Sizing
  • Reverse Engineering
  • System Testing and Acceptance Testing
Our end-to-end re-engineering process includes:
  • Assessing the customer's existing applications
  • Identifying a strategic solution
  • Executing the Project
  • Implementation and Transition
  • Performance issues
  • Additional enhancements in functionality
