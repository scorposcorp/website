Vision
Scorpos vision is to become a knowledge based organization in the IT sector, to deliver software solutions, services and products to customers globally. We aim to be the one of the most sought after organization’s for its customers, business associates and stakeholders while fulfilling its social responsibilities.
Mission
To provide premier information technology, systems engineering & software services support to our clients for maximum return on investment and sustainable strategic advantages. Combine management consulting with comprehensive, state-of-the-art IT and systems engineering services to expand knowledge, improve efficiency, increase effectiveness, and reduce program costs to ultimately advance the missions for all our customers.
