Associates  
FORMTEK, a Lockheed Martin Global Telecommunications Enterprise Solutions U.S. business unit is a leading global provider of Internet & client-server based Document & Content Management software products and services. Established in 1983 as a spin-off from Carnegie Mellon University’s computer aided design laboratory. FORMTEK provided enterprise-wide information management software alongwith engineering services for legacy systems integration, process re-engineering, and process automation. It also builds mission critical document and content management software solutions that address the business needs of today's eBusiness digital economy.

Currently, FORMTEK:ORION software solution successfully supports the F-16 and F-22 Fighter Enterprise programs at LMTAS. FORMTEK:ORION software changes the method by which LMTAS delivers data to international suppliers and co-producers.
