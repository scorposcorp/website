Training
 


 
 
   
 
Scorpos conducts a comprehensive program for all new employees to enrich them with adequate skills / knowledge, enabling them to perform well in their jobs. Here‚ you will play a critical role in delivering solutions that help businesses perform. With projects from around the globe‚ you will be exposed to domain‚ regional and technology challenges‚ adding to an all-round career experience.
Training Framework:
For any organization to achieve its goal the three most important factors required are People, Process and Tools & technology.

At Scorpos the most important factor is its People i.e. Scorpians. For Scorpians. To be best they need to continuously improve on three aspects

Soft Skills – to manage
Tools & Technology – to work
Processes – to bring all on the same page

We conduct a wide variety of soft skills training like Communication Skills, Presentation Skills, Client Management Skills, International Business Etiquette, Team Building Skills, Interviewing Skills, etc, apart from specialized training through external partners on Leadership, Management Development, Competency Mapping, etc. We at Scorpos encourage employees to take up educational programs so as to enhance their professional skills and individual competence

Mentoring
The Mentoring process at Scorpos focuses on the orientation of new employees. This process includes:
  • Guidance from a mentor and learning from their experiences
  • Access to information about various projects across Solution Centers
  • Opportunities to share new ideas, discuss common issues and arrive at suitable solutions
  • Guidance, encouragement and exposure to decision-making and leadership qualities
  • Smooth transition into the current role
